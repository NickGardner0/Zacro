# Code of conduct

Zacro welcomes thoughtful questions, bug reports, and contributions from people
of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Treat other contributors with respect. Critique the work and explain your reasoning.
- Keep discussions relevant, constructive, and considerate of people's time.
- Do not post harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, or private information.
- Share only logs, screenshots, and account data you are authorized to publish.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
these expectations are violated. GitHub's reporting tools are available for
abuse, harassment, and private-information disclosures.
